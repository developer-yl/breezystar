Privacy Policy

Last updated September 12, 2026

Purpose

BreezyStar operates a private reporting dashboard for the account owner. The dashboard combines App Store information with Google AdMob reporting so the owner can monitor app performance in one place.

Google user data we access

When the owner authorizes Google access, the dashboard requests permission to read AdMob account and reporting information. This may include app identifiers, estimated earnings, ad requests, impressions, clicks, and related performance metrics.

How we use Google user data

Google user data is used only to retrieve and display the authorized owner's AdMob reports. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and we do not sell it.

Storage and sharing

Authorization credentials and retrieved reporting information are used by the private dashboard operated by the account owner. We do not share Google user data with third parties except when required by law or necessary to protect the security of the service.

Retention and deletion

Reporting information may be retained locally for dashboard history and troubleshooting. The owner can remove locally stored reporting data and authorization credentials at any time. Google access can also be revoked from the Google Account permissions page.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect authorization credentials and reporting information. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Limited use

BreezyStar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support link provided on the relevant app's App Store listing.
